01 About Intracon

Sound advice for complex transport projects.

Established in 2013, The Intracon Group Pty Ltd is a specialist transport infrastructure consultancy based in Brisbane, Queensland.

We provide strategic, technical and delivery-focused advice across major transport projects, combining strong engineering fundamentals with practical experience in multidisciplinary project environments.

Our approach is built around clear decision-making, risk management, project accountability and commercially practical outcomes.
